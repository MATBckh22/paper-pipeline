# Method-defending language patterns for the frame linter (lexical stage).
# One case-insensitive regex per line; '#' starts a comment.
# A hit is a CANDIDATE - the judge stage adjudicates each one. Extend this
# list whenever the judge confirms a phrasing the list missed.
\bwe\s+(chose|choose|selected|select|opted|decided)\b
\bthis\s+(approach|method|design|choice|configuration)\s+(allows|enables|ensures|provides|offers)\b
\bin\s+order\s+to\s+(measure|capture|ensure|achieve|obtain)\b
\bmotivat\w*\s+(our|the)\s+(use|choice|adoption|selection)\b
\bwe\s+(use|adopt|employ|rely\s+on)\b.*\bbecause\b
\bwas\s+(chosen|selected|adopted)\s+(for|because|due\s+to|owing\s+to)\b
\bjustif(?:y|ies|ied|ication)\b
\bthe\s+rationale\s+(for|behind)\b
\b(allows|enables)\s+us\s+to\b
\bso\s+that\s+we\s+(can|could)\b
\bis\s+well[- ]suited\s+(for|to)\s+(our|the\s+present)\b
\bfor\s+this\s+reason,?\s+we\b
\bour\s+(choice|selection|adoption)\s+of\b
